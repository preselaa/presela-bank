# Presela

Everyday apparel with easy silhouettes and a consistent studio catalog. Lead with editorial photography, then let shoppers browse quickly. Use deep navy #101e3a for prominent surfaces, Presela navy #102c69 for controls and links, ice blue #eef5fc for information panels, and white for product cards. Use Horizon Inter with stronger contrast between editorial headlines and shopping copy. Use 16px mobile and 24px desktop gutters, a 1200px content width, and familiar keyboard-accessible product and cart controls.

On product pages, prioritize the gallery, price, size, and add-to-cart action. Use actual availability, payment information, reviews, and policy terms. Keep Shipping Protection optional in the cart drawer. Use Shopify's native product discovery, account history, and storefront cart.
